TF-IDF WEIGHTING CONCEPT & INFORMATION RETRIEVAL FOUNDATION:
TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) measures how important a word
or bigram is to a message within the corpus. It combines two factors:
1. Term Frequency (TF): Accounts for how many times a word occurs in a specific SMS.
2. Inverse Document Frequency (IDF): Decreases the weight of terms that appear in
   almost every message (e.g. 'ok', 'good'), while heavily boosting words that appear
   frequently only in specific contexts (e.g. 'claim', 'urgent', 'txt', 'prize').
This transformation yields a sparse vector representation where discriminative spam
indicators stand out distinctly from everyday interpersonal dialogue.
